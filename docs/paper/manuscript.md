## Scope reminder (do not overclaim)

The paper is **MIMIC-IV benchmark-v2 only** and **ontology-centered**. The detector and the
generative term (`Sgen`) are transparent **negative results**; external validation is
**blocked** by an eICU schema mismatch and is future work. Every headline number is backed
by an aggregate artifact under `artifacts/phase7/`.
